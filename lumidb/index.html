07Product film
Work area iii.

Product film.

The homepage hero, for the product brand — not a founder story. With brand visuals available as a companion deliverable.

01What you get

One finished product film, 60–90 seconds, plus a cutdown for social in two aspect ratios.

Optional add-on

A library of brand visual assets produced alongside the film — roughly ten stills and short looping animations.

The production already generates the raw material.

Optional add-on

Technical video, two to three minutes.

02What it takes

The messaging foundation completed.

In-house production. Fifteen to twenty workdays over four to six weeks.

Outsourced production for the technical video. Five to seven workdays plus production budget, over four to six weeks.

Specialty skills like sound design and color grading are outsourced.

03What it unlocks

The homepage stops doing the explaining work alone.

The film carries weight in cold outreach, events, and partner conversations.

With the asset add-on, the site and social stop looking like a technical product page — and start looking like a brand worth paying attention to.
